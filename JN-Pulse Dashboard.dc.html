A quiet desktop window on your JobNimbus data

A chromeless app window — launched via msedge --app — that opens on your local backup DB in under a second. You picked the chat-first layout: the ask box is the centre of gravity, glanceable stats frame it, and richer charts sit below. Every screen here is one state of that same window.

Styled in Nocturne — dark ground, blurple accent, Inter. Numbers are illustrative sample data for the mock; the real app shows only what a SQL query returns, per your honesty rule. One warm amber is added purely for alerts (stale sync, overdue) since Nocturne is otherwise mono. Open Tweaks to flip the accent to Pro24 red or hide the assistant's query trace.
